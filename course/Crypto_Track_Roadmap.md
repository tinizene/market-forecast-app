# Crypto Track — Full Chapter Roadmap

*Locked structure as of this point in development. Mirrors the proven Forex-track arc (mechanics → reading the market → risk → psychology → case studies → professional process), adapted to crypto's actual failure modes: custody loss, exchange collapse, scams, and volatility an order of magnitude beyond FX. Builds on Foundations Chapters 1–3 (universal concepts, not repeated here) and cross-references the Forex track where a concept genuinely transfers (leverage math, position sizing, the six-pillar thesis) rather than re-teaching it.*

*Voice: same honest skepticism as everywhere else — crypto is the vertical with the most hype and the most fraud, so the "verify the claim" thread runs hotter here than in any other track. No price predictions, no "to the moon," no laser eyes. Where the evidence is contested (store-of-value claims, on-chain analytics, diversification benefits that vanish in crises), the lesson says so.*

3. Consensus: Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake — mining, staking, energy, and the 51% attack — ✅ complete
4. Wallets, Keys & Custody — public/private keys, seed phrases, hot vs cold, and "not your keys, not your coins" taught as a trade-off, not a slogan — ✅ complete

6. Transactions, Fees & Finality — gas, confirmations, why "sent" isn't "settled," and fee spikes — ✅ complete
7. Stablecoins — pegs, reserves, and the difference between "backed" and "algorithmic" (the Terra teaser that Chapter 5 pays off) — ✅ complete
