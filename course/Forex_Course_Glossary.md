## "The Foundation of Money and Trade" Track Terms

### Forex / FX
- **EN:** The foreign exchange market — where currencies are bought and sold.
- **FR:** Forex / marché des changes
- **PT:** Forex / mercado cambial
- **SW:** Forex ⚠ (kept as-is; widely used in Swahili trading contexts)

### Currency Pair
- **EN:** Two currencies quoted against each other, e.g., EUR/USD.
- **FR:** Paire de devises
- **PT:** Par de moedas
- **SW:** Jozi la sarafu

### Base Currency
- **EN:** The first currency listed in a pair.
- **FR:** Devise de base
- **PT:** Moeda base
- **SW:** Sarafu ya msingi

### Quote Currency
- **EN:** The second currency listed in a pair.
- **FR:** Devise de cotation
- **PT:** Moeda cotada
- **SW:** Sarafu ya bei ⚠

### Bid Price
- **EN:** The price a buyer will pay for a currency.
- **FR:** Cours acheteur (bid)
- **PT:** Preço de compra (bid)
- **SW:** Bei ya ununuzi (bid)

### Ask Price
- **EN:** The price a seller will accept for a currency.
- **FR:** Cours vendeur (ask)
- **PT:** Preço de venda (ask)
- **SW:** Bei ya uuzaji (ask)

### Spread
- **EN:** The gap between the bid and ask price — effectively the cost of a trade.
- **FR:** Spread / écart
- **PT:** Spread
- **SW:** Spread ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Major Pairs
- **EN:** The most heavily traded pairs, always involving the US dollar (e.g., EUR/USD).
- **FR:** Paires majeures
- **PT:** Pares principais
- **SW:** Jozi kuu

### Minor Pairs
- **EN:** Pairs between two major currencies that exclude the US dollar (e.g., EUR/GBP).
- **FR:** Paires mineures
- **PT:** Pares menores
- **SW:** Jozi ndogo

### Exotic Pairs
- **EN:** A major currency paired with a smaller/emerging-market currency.
- **FR:** Paires exotiques
- **PT:** Pares exóticos
- **SW:** Jozi adimu ⚠

### Bull Market
- **EN:** A sustained period of rising prices.
- **FR:** Marché haussier
- **PT:** Mercado em alta
- **SW:** Soko linalopanda

### Bear Market
- **EN:** A sustained period of falling prices.
- **FR:** Marché baissier
- **PT:** Mercado em baixa
- **SW:** Soko linaloshuka

### Central Bank
- **EN:** A country's institution that sets monetary policy and interest rates.
- **FR:** Banque centrale
- **PT:** Banco central
- **SW:** Benki kuu

### Broker
- **EN:** A licensed firm that gives traders access to the market and executes their orders.
- **FR:** Courtier
- **PT:** Corretora
- **SW:** Wakala / dalali ⚠

### Pip
- **EN:** The smallest standard price move in a currency pair — usually the fourth decimal place.
- **FR:** Pip (kept as-is)
- **PT:** Pip (kept as-is)
- **SW:** Pip ⚠ (kept as-is; no established equivalent)

### Lot
- **EN:** A standardized trade size (standard = 100,000 units, mini = 10,000, micro = 1,000).
- **FR:** Lot
- **PT:** Lote
- **SW:** Lot ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Position Size
- **EN:** How much of a currency you're buying or selling in a single trade.
- **FR:** Taille de position
- **PT:** Tamanho da posição
- **SW:** Ukubwa wa nafasi

### Leverage
- **EN:** Borrowed buying power that lets a trader control a larger position than their own capital alone would allow.
- **FR:** Effet de levier
- **PT:** Alavancagem
- **SW:** Leverage ⚠ (kept as-is; common in East African trading communities)

### Margin
- **EN:** The trader's own capital held as collateral for a leveraged position.
- **FR:** Marge
- **PT:** Margem
- **SW:** Margin ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Margin Call
- **EN:** A broker's demand for more funds when an account's value falls too low to support open positions.
- **FR:** Appel de marge
- **PT:** Chamada de margem
- **SW:** Wito wa margin ⚠

---

### Barter
- **EN:** Trading goods or services directly for other goods or services, without using money.
- **FR:** Troc
- **PT:** Escambo
- **SW:** Kubadilishana bidhaa

### Double Coincidence of Wants
- **EN:** The requirement that two people each have exactly what the other wants, at the same time, for a barter trade to happen.
- **FR:** Double coïncidence des besoins
- **PT:** Dupla coincidência de desejos
- **SW:** Msuguano wa mahitaji sawia ⚠

### Medium of Exchange
- **EN:** Something widely accepted in trade for goods and services.
- **FR:** Moyen d'échange
- **PT:** Meio de troca
- **SW:** Njia ya kubadilishana bidhaa ⚠

### Store of Value
- **EN:** Something that holds its purchasing power over time.
- **FR:** Réserve de valeur
- **PT:** Reserva de valor
- **SW:** Hifadhi ya thamani

### Unit of Account
- **EN:** A common measure that lets people compare the value of different things.
- **FR:** Unité de compte
- **PT:** Unidade de conta
- **SW:** Kipimo cha thamani ⚠

### Scarcity
- **EN:** Limited supply — a key trait that let gold hold value as money.
- **FR:** Rareté
- **PT:** Escassez
- **SW:** Uhaba

---

### Debasement
- **EN:** Secretly reducing the precious-metal content of a coin while presenting it as full value.
- **FR:** Altération de la monnaie
- **PT:** Aviltamento da moeda
- **SW:** Kupunguza thamani ya sarafu ⚠

### IOU (Promissory Note)
- **EN:** A paper document promising the holder can redeem it for a real asset (e.g., gold) on demand.
- **FR:** Billet à ordre / reconnaissance de dette
- **PT:** Nota promissória
- **SW:** Hati ya deni ⚠

### Fractional Reserve Banking
- **EN:** A banking system where a bank holds only a fraction of deposits in reserve and lends out or issues notes against the rest.
- **FR:** Système de réserves fractionnaires
- **PT:** Sistema de reservas fracionárias
- **SW:** Mfumo wa akiba ya sehemu ⚠

### Bank Run
- **EN:** A situation where many depositors try to withdraw funds at once, faster than the bank can pay out.
- **FR:** Panique bancaire / ruée bancaire
- **PT:** Corrida bancária
- **SW:** Wateja kukimbilia benki kutoa fedha ⚠

### Central Bank
- **EN:** A country's official institution responsible for issuing its currency and regulating the banking system.
- **FR:** Banque centrale
- **PT:** Banco central
- **SW:** Benki kuu
*(Already introduced in Chapter 1.1 terms — repeated here for context; do not re-translate differently.)*

### Bretton Woods System
- **EN:** The 1944 agreement under which most currencies were pegged to the U.S. dollar, itself pegged to gold.
- **FR:** Système de Bretton Woods
- **PT:** Sistema de Bretton Woods
- **SW:** Mfumo wa Bretton Woods ⚠ (proper noun, kept largely as-is)

### Peg (Pegged Currency)
- **EN:** A currency whose value is fixed, or held within a narrow band, relative to another currency.
- **FR:** Ancrage de change / devise ancrée
- **PT:** Câmbio atrelado
- **SW:** Kufunga thamani ya sarafu ⚠

### Convertibility
- **EN:** The ability to exchange a currency for a fixed amount of another asset (e.g., gold) on demand.
- **FR:** Convertibilité
- **PT:** Convertibilidade
- **SW:** Uwezo wa kubadilisha sarafu kuwa dhahabu ⚠

### Triffin Dilemma
- **EN:** The conflict a reserve-currency country faces between supplying global liquidity and maintaining trust in its currency.
- **FR:** Dilemme de Triffin
- **PT:** Dilema de Triffin
- **SW:** Tatizo la Triffin ⚠ (proper noun, kept largely as-is)

### Nixon Shock
- **EN:** The August 1971 announcement suspending the U.S. dollar's convertibility to gold.
- **FR:** Choc Nixon
- **PT:** Choque de Nixon
- **SW:** Mshtuko wa Nixon ⚠ (proper noun, kept largely as-is)

### Floating Exchange Rate
- **EN:** An exchange rate set by open market supply and demand, rather than fixed by government agreement.
- **FR:** Taux de change flottant
- **PT:** Taxa de câmbio flutuante
- **SW:** Kiwango cha ubadilishaji kinachoelea ⚠

### Fiat Currency
- **EN:** Money valuable because a government declares it legal tender and people trust it — not because it's backed by a physical commodity.
- **FR:** Monnaie fiduciaire
- **PT:** Moeda fiduciária
- **SW:** Sarafu ya imani (fiat) ⚠

### Reserve Currency
- **EN:** A currency widely held by governments and institutions worldwide for international trade and finance.
- **FR:** Monnaie de réserve
- **PT:** Moeda de reserva
- **SW:** Sarafu ya akiba

---

### Gold Standard
- **EN:** A monetary system in which a country's currency is directly backed by, and convertible into, a fixed quantity of gold.
- **FR:** Étalon-or
- **PT:** Padrão-ouro
- **SW:** Kiwango cha dhahabu

### Gold Exchange Standard
- **EN:** An interwar variant of the gold standard mixing actual gold reserves with reserves of gold-convertible currencies.
- **FR:** Étalon de change-or
- **PT:** Padrão câmbio-ouro
- **SW:** Kiwango cha ubadilishaji wa dhahabu ⚠

### Monetary Policy
- **EN:** The actions a central bank takes to manage a country's money supply and interest rates.
- **FR:** Politique monétaire
- **PT:** Política monetária
- **SW:** Sera ya fedha

### Deflation
- **EN:** A general decline in prices across an economy over time — the opposite of inflation.
- **FR:** Déflation
- **PT:** Deflação
- **SW:** Kushuka kwa bei

### Devaluation
- **EN:** A deliberate reduction in the official value of a currency.
- **FR:** Dévaluation
- **PT:** Desvalorização
- **SW:** Kushuka kwa thamani ya sarafu

---

### Risk
- **EN:** The possibility that a decision doesn't go as intended, exposing you to loss.
- **FR:** Risque
- **PT:** Risco
- **SW:** Hatari

### Risk Management
- **EN:** Identifying a risk, judging whether it's worth taking, and controlling its potential cost.
- **FR:** Gestion du risque
- **PT:** Gestão de risco
- **SW:** Usimamizi wa hatari

### Risk Acceptance
- **EN:** Taking on a risk's full potential downside, with no limit.
- **FR:** Acceptation du risque
- **PT:** Aceitação de risco
- **SW:** Kukubali hatari

### Risk Avoidance
- **EN:** Choosing not to take an action because the risk outweighs the benefit.
- **FR:** Évitement du risque
- **PT:** Evitação de risco
- **SW:** Kuepuka hatari

### Risk Limitation
- **EN:** Accepting a risk but capping how much it can cost you — what a stop-loss does mechanically.
- **FR:** Limitation du risque
- **PT:** Limitação de risco
- **SW:** Kupunguza hatari

### Risk Transference
- **EN:** Paying another party to take on your risk (e.g., insurance, or options/hedging in trading).
- **FR:** Transfert du risque
- **PT:** Transferência de risco
- **SW:** Uhamishaji wa hatari ⚠

### Entry Price
- **EN:** The price at which you open a position — where you actually buy or sell.
- **FR:** Prix d'entrée
- **PT:** Preço de entrada
- **SW:** Bei ya kuingia

### Stop-Loss
- **EN:** A predetermined price at which you exit a losing position, capping how much it can cost you.
- **FR:** Stop-loss (ordre de protection)
- **PT:** Stop-loss (ordem de proteção)
- **SW:** Stop-loss ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Take-Profit
- **EN:** A predetermined price at which you exit a winning position, locking in a gain.
- **FR:** Take-profit (ordre de prise de profit)
- **PT:** Take-profit (ordem de realização de lucro)
- **SW:** Take-profit ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Long Position
- **EN:** A position that profits if the asset's price goes up.
- **FR:** Position longue
- **PT:** Posição comprada / posição longa
- **SW:** Nafasi ndefu ⚠

### Short Position
- **EN:** A position that profits if the asset's price goes down.
- **FR:** Position courte / position vendeuse
- **PT:** Posição vendida / posição curta
- **SW:** Nafasi fupi ⚠

---

### Inflation
- **EN:** A general, sustained rise in prices across an economy, which reduces what a currency can buy.
- **FR:** Inflation
- **PT:** Inflação
- **SW:** Mfumko wa bei

### Demand-Pull Inflation
- **EN:** Inflation caused by demand growing faster than the economy's ability to supply goods and services.
- **FR:** Inflation par la demande
- **PT:** Inflação de demanda
- **SW:** Mfumko wa bei unaosababishwa na mahitaji ⚠

### Cost-Push Inflation
- **EN:** Inflation caused by rising production costs being passed on to consumers.
- **FR:** Inflation par les coûts
- **PT:** Inflação de custos
- **SW:** Mfumko wa bei unaosababishwa na gharama ⚠

### Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- **EN:** The most widely used measure of average price change across a household basket of goods and services.
- **FR:** Indice des prix à la consommation (IPC)
- **PT:** Índice de Preços ao Consumidor (IPC)
- **SW:** Kielezo cha Bei za Mlaji (CPI) ⚠

### Core Inflation
- **EN:** Inflation measured after excluding volatile food and energy prices.
- **FR:** Inflation sous-jacente
- **PT:** Inflação subjacente
- **SW:** Mfumko wa bei wa msingi ⚠

### Value Stock
- **EN:** A share trading below what its current earnings and cash flow arguably justify.
- **FR:** Action de valeur
- **PT:** Ação de valor
- **SW:** Hisa ya thamani ⚠

### Growth Stock
- **EN:** A share priced mainly on the expectation of much larger future earnings.
- **FR:** Action de croissance
- **PT:** Ação de crescimento
- **SW:** Hisa ya ukuaji ⚠

---

### Portfolio
- **EN:** The complete collection of everything you hold, treated as one connected whole.
- **FR:** Portefeuille
- **PT:** Carteira / portfólio
- **SW:** Mkusanyiko wa uwekezaji ⚠

### Diversification
- **EN:** Spreading exposure across different assets, sectors, regions, or markets so no single holding dominates the result.
- **FR:** Diversification
- **PT:** Diversificação
- **SW:** Mseto wa uwekezaji ⚠

### Correlation
- **EN:** A measure of how closely two assets' prices tend to move in relation to each other.
- **FR:** Corrélation
- **PT:** Correlação
- **SW:** Uhusiano wa mabadiliko ya bei ⚠

### Diversifier
- **EN:** An asset with an average positive but imperfect correlation to your existing holdings.
- **FR:** Diversificateur
- **PT:** Diversificador
- **SW:** Kigezo cha mseto ⚠

### Hedge
- **EN:** An asset with an average zero or negative correlation to another asset, across all conditions.
- **FR:** Couverture (hedge)
- **PT:** Hedge / proteção
- **SW:** Kinga ya hatari ⚠

### Safe Haven
- **EN:** An asset that holds zero or negative correlation specifically during periods of market stress.
- **FR:** Valeur refuge
- **PT:** Ativo de refúgio
- **SW:** Hifadhi salama ⚠

---

### Line Chart
- **EN:** Connects closing prices over time with a single line.
- **FR:** Graphique en ligne
- **PT:** Gráfico de linha
- **SW:** Chati ya mstari

### Bar Chart
- **EN:** Shows open/high/low/close per period as a bar with open/close ticks.
- **FR:** Graphique en barres
- **PT:** Gráfico de barras
- **SW:** Chati ya baa

### Candlestick Chart
- **EN:** Same open/high/low/close data as a bar chart, with a colored body for fast visual reading.
- **FR:** Graphique en chandeliers
- **PT:** Gráfico de candlestick
- **SW:** Chati ya candlestick ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Uptrend
- **EN:** A series of higher highs and higher lows.
- **FR:** Tendance haussière
- **PT:** Tendência de alta
- **SW:** Mwelekeo wa kupanda

### Downtrend
- **EN:** A series of lower highs and lower lows.
- **FR:** Tendance baissière
- **PT:** Tendência de baixa
- **SW:** Mwelekeo wa kushuka

### Support
- **EN:** A price level where falling prices have repeatedly reversed upward.
- **FR:** Support
- **PT:** Suporte
- **SW:** Kiwango cha msaada

### Resistance
- **EN:** A price level where rising prices have repeatedly reversed downward.
- **FR:** Résistance
- **PT:** Resistência
- **SW:** Kiwango cha upinzani

### Tape Reading
- **EN:** Reading real-time trade price, size, and volume to gauge buying/selling pressure as it happens.
- **FR:** Lecture du tape / lecture du ticker
- **PT:** Leitura de fita (tape reading)
- **SW:** Usomaji wa tepu ⚠ (kept partly as-is; niche historical term)

### Peer Review
- **EN:** Independent expert evaluation of a study's methodology and findings before publication.
- **FR:** Évaluation par les pairs
- **PT:** Revisão por pares
- **SW:** Ukaguzi wa kitaalamu ⚠

### ISO 4217
- **EN:** The international standard defining three-letter alphabetic codes (and numeric codes) for the world's currencies.
- **FR:** ISO 4217 (kept as-is; proper noun/standard name)
- **PT:** ISO 4217 (kept as-is)
- **SW:** ISO 4217 ⚠ (kept as-is)

---

### Liquidity
- **EN:** How easily and quickly an asset can be bought or sold without significantly moving its price.
- **FR:** Liquidité
- **PT:** Liquidez
- **SW:** Ukwasi *(consistent with Foundations Ch1 usage — same term)*

### Major Currency
- **EN:** One of the handful of currencies dominating global forex trading volume (USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, AUD, CAD, CHF).
- **FR:** Devise majeure
- **PT:** Moeda principal
- **SW:** Sarafu kuu

### Major Currency Pair
- **EN:** A currency pair made up of two major currencies, almost always including the U.S. dollar.
- **FR:** Paire de devises majeure
- **PT:** Par de moedas principal
- **SW:** Jozi kuu la sarafu

### Minor Pair (Cross Pair)
- **EN:** A currency pair combining two major currencies, with no U.S. dollar involved (e.g., EUR/GBP, CAD/JPY). "Minor pair" and "cross pair" refer to the same thing.
- **FR:** Paire mineure / paire croisée
- **PT:** Par menor / par cruzado
- **SW:** Jozi dogo la sarafu ⚠

### Exotic Pair
- **EN:** A major currency paired with the currency of a smaller or emerging economy (e.g., USD/MXN, USD/TRY, USD/ZAR). Lower liquidity, wider spreads, higher volatility than majors or minors.
- **FR:** Paire exotique
- **PT:** Par exótico
- **SW:** Jozi geni la sarafu ⚠

---

### Pipette
- **EN:** A tenth of a pip — one decimal place further than the standard pip, used by many modern brokers for finer pricing precision.
- **FR:** Pipette (kept as-is)
- **PT:** Pipette (kept as-is)
- **SW:** Pipette ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Adverse Selection
- **EN:** In market-making, the risk that a counterparty knows more than you do and is trading on that advantage — a core reason bid-ask spreads exist.
- **FR:** Sélection adverse (anti-sélection)
- **PT:** Seleção adversa
- **SW:** Uteuzi hasi ⚠ (technical economics term, flagged for review)

---

### Position Sizing
- **EN:** Calculating exactly how much of a currency to buy or sell, based on account size, risk tolerance, and stop-loss distance. *(The process — distinct from "Position Size," the resulting amount, already defined above.)*
- **FR:** Dimensionnement de position
- **PT:** Dimensionamento de posição
- **SW:** Uwekaji wa ukubwa wa nafasi ⚠

### Kelly Criterion
- **EN:** A 1956 formula (John L. Kelly Jr., Bell Labs) for the mathematically optimal fraction of capital to risk on a bet or trade, to maximize long-term growth.
- **FR:** Critère de Kelly
- **PT:** Critério de Kelly
- **SW:** Kigezo cha Kelly ⚠ (proper noun, kept largely as-is)

---

### Margin Level
- **EN:** A live percentage measure of account equity relative to margin currently in use — (Equity ÷ Used Margin) × 100.
- **FR:** Niveau de marge
- **PT:** Nível de margem
- **SW:** Kiwango cha margin ⚠

---

### Market Order
- **EN:** An instruction to buy or sell immediately at the best available current price. Guarantees execution, not price.
- **FR:** Ordre au marché
- **PT:** Ordem a mercado
- **SW:** Agizo la soko

### Limit Order
- **EN:** An instruction to buy or sell only at a specified price or better. Guarantees price, not execution.
- **FR:** Ordre à cours limité
- **PT:** Ordem limitada
- **SW:** Agizo lenye kikomo

### Stop Order
- **EN:** An order that becomes a market order once price reaches a trigger level.
- **FR:** Ordre stop
- **PT:** Ordem stop
- **SW:** Agizo la stop

### Stop-Limit Order
- **EN:** A hybrid order that becomes a limit order (not a market order) once triggered.
- **FR:** Ordre stop-limite
- **PT:** Ordem stop-limitada
- **SW:** Agizo la stop-limit ⚠

### Trailing Stop
- **EN:** A stop-loss that automatically moves in the trader's favor as price moves favorably, never moving back.
- **FR:** Stop suiveur
- **PT:** Stop móvel
- **SW:** Stop inayosogea ⚠

### OCO Order (One-Cancels-the-Other)
- **EN:** Two linked orders where the execution of either one automatically cancels the other.
- **FR:** Ordre OCO
- **PT:** Ordem OCO
- **SW:** Agizo la OCO ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Slippage
- **EN:** The difference between the expected fill price of an order and its actual fill price.
- **FR:** Glissement (slippage)
- **PT:** Derrapagem (slippage)
- **SW:** Mteremko wa bei ⚠

---

### Technical Indicator
- **EN:** A calculation from a chart's price and/or volume data only, highlighting what's already visible in price.
- **FR:** Indicateur technique
- **PT:** Indicador técnico
- **SW:** Kiashiria cha kiufundi ⚠

### Simple Moving Average (SMA)
- **EN:** The average of the last N closing prices, recalculated each period as the window slides forward.
- **FR:** Moyenne mobile simple
- **PT:** Média móvel simples
- **SW:** Wastani unaosogea ⚠

### Moving Average Crossover
- **EN:** A signal generated when a shorter-period moving average crosses above or below a longer-period one.
- **FR:** Croisement de moyennes mobiles
- **PT:** Cruzamento de médias móveis
- **SW:** Mkatizo wa wastani unaosogea ⚠

### Whipsaw
- **EN:** A signal that reverses almost immediately after triggering, producing a small loss — the known weakness of crossover systems in sideways markets.
- **FR:** Faux signal (whipsaw)
- **PT:** Sinal falso (whipsaw)
- **SW:** Whipsaw ⚠ (kept as-is)

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- **EN:** An indicator measuring the distance between two moving averages, plotted as a line oscillating around zero — a formalization of the crossover.
- **FR:** MACD (kept as-is)
- **PT:** MACD (kept as-is)
- **SW:** MACD ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Bollinger Bands
- **EN:** A 20-period moving average with bands ±2 standard deviations above and below, widening with volatility. Developed by John Bollinger.
- **FR:** Bandes de Bollinger
- **PT:** Bandas de Bollinger
- **SW:** Mikanda ya Bollinger ⚠

### Standard Deviation
- **EN:** A statistical measure of how spread out values are around their average.
- **FR:** Écart type
- **PT:** Desvio padrão
- **SW:** Mchepuko wa kawaida ⚠

### Fat Tails
- **EN:** The empirical reality (Mandelbrot, 1963) that extreme price moves occur far more often than the normal distribution predicts.
- **FR:** Queues épaisses (fat tails)
- **PT:** Caudas gordas (fat tails)
- **SW:** Fat tails ⚠ (kept as-is; technical statistics term)

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **EN:** Wilder's 0–100 momentum oscillator measuring the relative size of recent gains vs. losses over 14 periods.
- **FR:** RSI (kept as-is)
- **PT:** RSI / Índice de Força Relativa
- **SW:** RSI ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Overbought / Oversold
- **EN:** Conventional RSI extremes — above 70 (one-sided recent buying) / below 30 (one-sided recent selling).
- **FR:** Suracheté / survendu
- **PT:** Sobrecomprado / sobrevendido
- **SW:** Kununuliwa kupita kiasi / kuuzwa kupita kiasi ⚠

### Fibonacci Retracement
- **EN:** A charting tool marking levels at 23.6/38.2/50/61.8/78.6% of a price move, watched as potential pullback zones. (The 50% level is convention, not a Fibonacci ratio.)
- **FR:** Retracement de Fibonacci
- **PT:** Retração de Fibonacci
- **SW:** Fibonacci retracement ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Mean Reversion
- **EN:** A trading approach betting that a stretched price will return toward its average — the opposite bet from trend-following.
- **FR:** Retour à la moyenne
- **PT:** Reversão à média
- **SW:** Kurudi kwenye wastani ⚠

---

### Fundamental Analysis
- **EN:** Evaluating a currency by the underlying economic forces that drive its value (rates, inflation, growth, policy), rather than chart patterns.
- **FR:** Analyse fondamentale
- **PT:** Analise fundamental
- **SW:** Uchambuzi wa msingi

### Base Interest Rate
- **EN:** A central bank's primary policy tool - effectively the price of money, shaping loan and savings rates across the economy.
- **FR:** Taux d'interet directeur
- **PT:** Taxa de juro base
- **SW:** Kiwango cha riba cha msingi ⚠

### Hawkish / Dovish
- **EN:** Hawkish = favouring higher rates to fight inflation; dovish = favouring lower rates to promote growth.
- **FR:** Restrictif / accommodant (hawkish / dovish)
- **PT:** Restritivo / expansionista (hawkish / dovish)
- **SW:** Hawkish / Dovish ⚠ (kept as-is; technical policy terms)

### Carry Trade
- **EN:** Borrowing or selling a low-interest-rate currency to buy a high-interest-rate one, aiming to earn the interest rate difference.
- **FR:** Operation de portage (carry trade)
- **PT:** Operacao de carry trade
- **SW:** Carry trade ⚠ (kept as-is)

### Uncovered Interest Parity (UIP)
- **EN:** The theory that a higher-interest-rate currency should depreciate by exactly enough to cancel its interest advantage - implying no free profit from chasing high rates.
- **FR:** Parite des taux d'interet non couverte
- **PT:** Paridade descoberta de taxas de juro
- **SW:** UIP ⚠ (kept as-is; technical economics term)

### Forward Premium Puzzle (Fama Puzzle)
- **EN:** The robust empirical finding that UIP fails - high-interest-rate currencies have historically tended to keep appreciating in the short run, contrary to theory (Fama, 1984).
- **FR:** Enigme de la prime de terme
- **PT:** Enigma do premio a prazo
- **SW:** Forward premium puzzle ⚠ (kept as-is)

---

### Economic Calendar
- **EN:** A schedule of upcoming economic data releases and policy announcements, listing time, currency affected, expected impact, and market forecast.
- **FR:** Calendrier economique
- **PT:** Calendario economico
- **SW:** Kalenda ya kiuchumi

### Forecast (Consensus)
- **EN:** The market's aggregated expectation for an upcoming data release - the benchmark the actual result is judged against.
- **FR:** Prevision (consensus)
- **PT:** Previsao (consenso)
- **SW:** Utabiri wa soko ⚠

### Announcement Surprise
- **EN:** The gap between a forecast and the actual released figure - the component that research shows actually moves exchange rates.
- **FR:** Surprise d'annonce
- **PT:** Surpresa do anuncio
- **SW:** Mshangao wa tangazo ⚠

### Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)
- **EN:** The monthly US employment report; one of the highest-impact scheduled releases in the forex market.
- **FR:** Emplois non agricoles (NFP)
- **PT:** Folha de pagamento nao agricola (NFP)
- **SW:** NFP ⚠ (kept as-is)

---

### Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTFA)
- **EN:** Examining the same currency pair across several chart timeframes - long, medium and short - before committing to a trade.
- **FR:** Analyse multi-horizons
- **PT:** Analise de multiplos periodos
- **SW:** Uchambuzi wa vipindi mbalimbali ⚠

### The Rule of Four
- **EN:** A convention for picking complementary timeframes: choose the medium first, then long is about four times it and short about a quarter of it.
- **FR:** La regle de quatre
- **PT:** A regra de quatro
- **SW:** Kanuni ya nne ⚠

### Timeframe-Shopping
- **EN:** Cycling through chart timeframes until one agrees with a trade you already wanted - a form of confirmation bias, not analysis.
- **FR:** Recherche d'horizon complaisant
- **PT:** Busca por periodo favoravel
- **SW:** Kutafuta kipindi kinachokubali ⚠

### Data Snooping
- **EN:** Searching a large set of possibilities for one that fits your data, then treating the winner as a genuine discovery when it may be a coincidence (Sullivan, Timmermann and White, 1999).
- **FR:** Exploration abusive des donnees (data snooping)
- **PT:** Garimpagem de dados (data snooping)
- **SW:** Data snooping ⚠ (kept as-is; technical statistics term)
