Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what data Scere Training collects, why, and how it's used. It applies to the web app at this domain and any installed PWA version of it.

What we collect

  • Local device storage: your selected instrument is stored only in your browser's local storage, never sent to our servers.
  • No account, no personal data: this MVP has no login and no server-side storage of anything about you. Quote, trend, and news requests pass through our proxy endpoints only to keep API keys off the client — nothing about you is logged there beyond standard hosting-provider request logs.

Third-party services we use

  • Alpha Vantage — equity-index-proxy quotes, price history, and published economic series (Treasury yield, Fed funds rate).
  • Frankfurter — FX reference rates (ECB data).
  • Financial news/newsletter RSS feeds (Investing.com, Federal Reserve, WSJ) — publicly published content, fetched server-side and relayed to the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, or phone number through the app itself.
  • We don't sell your data to third parties.
  • We don't execute trades or move money — this app is read-only market information.

Not financial advice

Nothing in this app is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security or instrument. All trend/volatility/momentum indicators are computed client-visible statistics derived from recent price history, not investment advice. See a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions.

This is a plain-language summary intended for transparency, not a substitute for legal advice. If you need this policy (or the app's financial-content disclaimers) reviewed for a specific jurisdiction's requirements, consult a lawyer before launching publicly.
