## 16. Historical Parallel Watch

**Historical Parallel: "1990 Gulf War Oil Shock + 1992 ERM Crisis Hybrid"**

The current environment (mid-August 2026) shares characteristics with **two historical periods**:

1. **1990 Gulf War Oil Shock (August 1990 – January 1991):**  
   Iraq's invasion of Kuwait (August 2, 1990) closed Kuwaiti oil production and threatened Saudi supply, spiking Brent from ~$18 to $40 (peak October 1990) before crashing back to $20 by January 1991 (post-liberation). **Parallel:** Strait of Hormuz closure (February 2026) is structurally similar to 1990 Kuwait closure — both removed ~20% of global oil supply. Oil spiked, then consolidated as markets adapted. **Divergence:** 1990 spike was **shorter** (~3 months peak-to-trough); 2026 Hormuz stalemate has lasted **5 months** (February–August), suggesting a more durable premium. **Lesson:** If Hormuz resolves suddenly (like Kuwait liberation in Jan 1991), expect **violent oil crash** (Brent $90 → $70 within weeks), unwinding commodity longs (AUD, NZD). If Hormuz drags on (no resolution through year-end), oil floor at $85–90 could persist for **quarters**, not months.
