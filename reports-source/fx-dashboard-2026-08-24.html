16. Historical Parallel Watch

Parallel: "The 2007 Carry-Trade Unwind — When VIX, Yen Strength, and Geopolitical Oil Converged."

In mid-2007, the global carry trade (borrow yen at 0.50%, invest in AUD/NZD/EM) was the dominant strategy. VIX was subdued (~12–14), equities near highs, oil rising (to $70–80 on demand growth), and the Fed easing (first cut Sept 2007). Sound familiar? By August 2007, cracks appeared: yen started strengthening (USD/JPY fell from 124 to 120), VIX spiked to 20+ on subprime fears, and oil rally turned geopolitical (Middle East tensions). Carry trades unwound violently in Aug-Sept 2007: AUD/JPY fell from 105 to 95 (–10%), NZD/JPY from 95 to 85 (–10%), and VIX hit 30 by November. The lesson: when yen strengthens AND VIX rises AND oil rally is geopolitically driven (not demand), carry trades unwind fast, regardless of rate differentials.

Today's parallel:

  • BoJ tightening (1.00%, hiking Sept) vs 2007 BoJ on hold (0.50%).
  • VIX rising (15.13, up from 14.2) vs 2007 VIX rising from ~12 to 20+.
  • Oil geopolitical rally ($94 on Iran/Hormuz) vs 2007 oil rally on Middle East.
  • Equities near highs (S&P 7,674) vs 2007 S&P near highs before Aug crash.
  • AUD/JPY under pressure (~111, testing support) vs 2007 AUD/JPY breakdown from 105.

Key difference: In 2007, the trigger was subprime mortgage crisis (exogenous financial shock). Today, the trigger is BoJ policy tightening (endogenous central-bank action), which is more predictable but still capable of unwinding carry. The parallel is not exact, but the pattern is similar enough to warrant caution.

What to watch: If VIX breaks 16 AND USD/JPY breaks 155 AND AUD/JPY breaks 109, it confirms the 2007-style carry unwind is underway. In that scenario, expect AUD/JPY to fall to 105, EUR/JPY to 175, GBP/JPY to 210, and VIX to 20+ within 2–4 weeks. Position accordingly: reduce carry exposure, add JPY/CHF longs, buy VIX calls.
