Historical parallel #1: October 1973 – January 1974 (Yom Kippur War / First Oil Shock)

  • Central banks tightening into an oil-driven inflation spike ✅
  • Stagflation risk (growth slowing, inflation rising) ✅

Difference from today:

Outcome in 1973–74: USD rallied (Fed out-hawked other CBs), equities crashed, recession followed. If 2026 rhymes, we'd expect USD strength, equity weakness, and FX vol to spike (carry trades unwind, safe havens bid).

Probability this parallel plays out: Moderate (40%).
