1 Market Regime & Executive Summary

Regime: The AI-valuation unwind deepens and concentrates in Japan, while a sharply repriced hawkish Fed collides with the widening Iran/Hormuz war — three separate forces, each strong enough to move markets alone, now overlapping.

Japan's Nikkei 225 sank as much as 4.4% intraday to roughly 63,896, briefly breaching 65,000 for the first time in about a month, driven by renewed AI-spending anxiety after TSMC lifted its 2026 capex guidance to $60-64bn. Kioxia plunged ~16%, Advantest and SoftBank each lost ~9%. Critically, South Korea's KOSPI closed down only 0.46% today versus yesterday's 7%+ plunge — a bifurcation shift: this is concentrating in Japan specifically, not remaining a uniform regional rout. On Wall Street Thursday, the S&P 500 fell 0.51% and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.47%; Nasdaq 100 futures were a further ~0.3% softer into Friday.

The Iran/Hormuz war extended into a sixth consecutive night of US strikes; its regional footprint widened today — Qatar intercepted a missile, Kuwait responded to threats, Bahrain sounded sirens — even as Brent eased marginally to ~$85.00 from Wednesday's $85.90 high, and gold held near $3,984-3,986/oz, still short of $4,000.

Most consequential for this report's own book: Fed September hike odds surged from sub-17% to a 44.5%-56% range across sources found this session, fueled by Thursday's strong Initial Claims (208K) and Philadelphia Fed (41.4, ~4x consensus) plus today's improved Michigan sentiment. DXY firmed to ~100.78 — a genuine hawkish repricing that directly threatens this tracker's open Short USD/JPY idea.
